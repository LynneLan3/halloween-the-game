# Editorial method

Guide pages on this site may draw on:

- official announcements, store pages, or documentation
- firsthand gameplay notes and screenshots we can describe accurately
- community research that we re-check before publishing
- manual review before a page is linked from the Hub
- follow-up edits when facts, patches, or supporting evidence change

We separate reported facts, firsthand observations, recommendations, and UNKNOWNs. We do not present a dedicated editorial desk or legal review team that does not exist, and we do not fill evidence gaps with guesses.
