# About this site

This is an independent fan guide and reference site for **Halloween: The Game**, published by IllFonic.

The site covers maps, characters, Michael Myers, multiplayer, story challenges, PC questions, and release updates. The goal is to publish specific, verifiable information that helps players find answers quickly — not to copy marketing copy or guess at details we cannot support.

This is an independent fan resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IllFonic. If something on a guide page is marked as unconfirmed or UNKNOWN, treat it as an evidence boundary until a reliable source or current-build observation supports an update.
