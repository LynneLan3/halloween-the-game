# Privacy

This page is informational and not legal advice. It describes the services currently configured for the production site.

This site uses Google Analytics 4 on the production hostname to understand aggregate usage, such as page views, outbound link clicks, approximate location, device type, and referral source. The site uses this information to improve guide coverage and measure which sources, evidence links, and content links are useful.

Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar browser storage when it loads. Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights are also enabled for production measurement and performance monitoring. These services may process technical information such as page URL, device/browser details, network information, and interaction or performance data.

The site loads a third-party advertising script on guide pages when advertising is enabled. The advertising provider and hosting providers may process routine technical data, such as IP address, user agent, device information, and request data, to deliver, measure, and secure the site. Third parties may place or read cookies or use similar technologies for analytics or advertising according to their own policies.

The site does not sell visitor data. For Google's explanation of how partner sites and apps use data when they use Google services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
