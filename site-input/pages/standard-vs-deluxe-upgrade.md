# Halloween: The Game Standard vs Deluxe — Upgrade & Worth It?

Quick answer

- Price: Standard is $39.99; Digital Deluxe is $59.99 — exactly a $20 difference. The $20 buys the Inmate Michael Myers skin plus the civilians Richard and Alexis and other Deluxe/preorder extras.
- Upgrade: A Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU is available at launch so Standard owners can add Deluxe content later; the upgrade price per storefront is unannounced and should not be assumed to equal $20.
- Early Access & preorder bonuses: Digital Deluxe preorders get Early Access starting Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. Official clarification (Sep 3) says purchasing Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window still grants Early Access and preorder bonuses through Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT — see [Advance Access timing]({{page:early-access-release-time}}).
- Missables: The Phantom Michael Myers skin is a preorder-only item and is not restored by the Deluxe Upgrade.

See the [characters guide]({{page:characters-hub}}) and the Michael skin list at [Michael Myers skins]({{page:michael-myers-skins}}).

Official clarification (Sep 3) states purchases of Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window still receive Early Access and preorder bonuses through Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. ## Which edition should you buy — conditional recommendation

- Buy Digital Deluxe ($59.99) if:
  - You want Early Access on Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT and the Deluxe cosmetics/characters (Inmate Michael, Richard, Alexis).
  - You want to guarantee preorder-only items (note: Phantom is preorder-only — see Common mistakes).
  - You plan to preorder or purchase Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window (official clarification confirms purchases during that window still get Early Access and preorder bonuses through Sep 8 9 AM PT).
- Buy Standard ($39.99) if:
  - You only want the base game and will play after the full launch (Sep 8).
  - You want to defer the decision and are willing to accept that some preorder items (Phantom) cannot be recovered later.
- Unsure now:
  - Buy Standard and decide at launch: the Deluxe Upgrade SKU will be available at launch so you can add Deluxe content later, but the upgrade price is unannounced and preorder-only items remain missable for non-preorders.

## Upgrade details — can you upgrade later?

1. There is a Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU available at launch that allows Standard owners to acquire Deluxe content without rebuying the full Digital Deluxe edition.
2. The upgrade grants the Deluxe civilians and the Inmate Michael skin, plus the other Deluxe extras listed by IllFonic.
3. The Deluxe Upgrade price is unannounced — do not assume it equals the $20 list difference between the two full SKUs.
4. Important: the Deluxe Upgrade does **not** restore the Phantom Michael Myers preorder skin. If Phantom is important to you, acquire it during the preorder / Early Access window.

## Early Access (Advance Access) — what changed

- Early Access (Advance Access) schedule: Early Access begins Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT; full launch is Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. See [Advance Access timing]({{page:early-access-release-time}}).
- Official clarification (published Sep 3): Purchasing Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window still grants Early Access and all preorder bonuses through Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT.
- What the clarification does not change: the Phantom skin remains a preorder-only reward and is not restorable by the Deluxe Upgrade. The exact upgrade price remains unannounced.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade will give you the Phantom skin. It will not — Phantom is preorder-only and cannot be recovered via the upgrade.
- Assuming Standard includes Early Access. Standard does not list Early Access.
- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade price is already known or equal to $20. The upgrade price per storefront is unannounced.
- Assuming any post–Early Access purchase policy (outside the published Early Access window) — do not infer post‑Sep 8 upgrade/early‑access behavior beyond official timings.

## What happens next

- If you want Sep 4 Early Access or the Phantom preorder item: preorder Digital Deluxe before the preorder/Early Access window closes.
- If you buy Standard now and later decide you want Deluxe: purchase the Deluxe Edition Upgrade at launch (price unknown) to add the Inmate skin and civilians, but you will not recover the Phantom preorder item if you missed the preorder window.
