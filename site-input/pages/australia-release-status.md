# Is Halloween: The Game Banned in Australia? Current Release Status

Confirmed

- The Australian Classification Board issued a Refused Classification (RC) determination for the base listing of Halloween: The Game in August 2026. The Board’s stated reason: the game links illicit drug use to a gameplay advantage or reward (Classification Board statement reported by Press Start). [Last Verified: 2026-08-29]
- IllFonic publicly said AU/NZ digital and physical sale was blocked after the RC decision (publisher communication summarized in reporting).
- Global digital release plans remain in IllFonic’s storefront messaging: the game has a digital release date of September 8, 2026, with an Advance Access window beginning Sep 4, 2026 for eligible preorders (see preorder FAQ and official storefronts).
- SKU pricing and contents are confirmed by IllFonic/store pages: Standard $39.99; Digital Deluxe $59.99. The Digital Deluxe is listed to include specific cosmetics and preorder bonuses; the Phantom Michael Myers pre-order skin is explicitly described as a pre-order exclusive that is not restored by the post-launch Deluxe upgrade.

Not Announced

- No official public reclassification or change to the RC status of the base game has been announced by the Australian Classification Board or IllFonic as of the Last Verified date. Do not treat secondary or storefront metadata as an official reversal.
- There is no publisher confirmation that purchasing the Digital Deluxe after the preorder period will grant the same Early Access gameplay window as Deluxe preorders that were explicitly listed as eligible.

Unknown / Needs launch verification

- A separate Digital Deluxe IARC listing showing R18+ has been reported by secondary sources. This listing is not the same as an Australian Classification Board reclassification of the base game; whether that SKU will be permitted for sale in Australia at launch is unknown and requires official confirmation from the Classification Board or IllFonic.
- Final, SKU‑specific sale availability in Australia at launch (digital and physical) is unknown pending either an official reclassification, a changed SKU submission, or a publisher workaround. Do not assume the RC decision has been overturned.
- Whether any storefronts will remove or replace restricted content for AU customers (e.g., a modified SKU) is not confirmed.

What this means for Australian buyers

- As of Last Verified (2026-08-29): the legally effective outcome is that the published base listing has been Refused Classification and IllFonic said sales into AU/NZ were blocked. That means the base game cannot be legally sold in Australia unless and until the Classification Board reclassifies it or a compliant SKU is submitted and approved.
- A reported separate Digital Deluxe IARC entry with R18+ should not be read as automatic approval for sale in Australia. Only an official Classification Board decision or an explicit IllFonic announcement stating the base game or a specific SKU is cleared for sale constitutes a change to the status.

Where to watch for updates

- Official sources to monitor: IllFonic / Halloween: The Game official site and FAQ ({{hub}}), the Australian Classification Board public decisions page, and major storefront entries (Steam/Epic/PlayStation/Xbox). For Advance Access and preorder eligibility details see our {{page:early-access-release-time}} and SKU comparison info at {{page:standard-vs-deluxe-upgrade}}.
