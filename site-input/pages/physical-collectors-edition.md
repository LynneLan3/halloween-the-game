# Halloween: The Game Physical & Collector’s Editions — Price, Contents & Release Date

Quick answer
- Physical release date: October 6, 2026 — available on PS5 and Xbox Series X.  
- Prices: Standard Edition $39.99; Limited Collector’s Edition $149.99.  
- The physical product ships with the game on disc.  
- Standard includes Michael Myers Clown skin Advance Access, 2 Civilian cosmetics Advance Access, and a poster.  
- Collector’s includes Clown + Samhain Michael Advance Access, 4 Civilian cosmetics Advance Access, poster, Shape Jump Variant Action Figure, lenticular art, and "more surprises" (official wording).  
- Advance Access cosmetics are also earnable later via in‑game challenges (they are early-access, not permanent physical-only locks).  
- Phantom Michael is a Digital Pre‑order Exclusive and is not included with physical editions.

Digital vs. physical timing
1. Digital Early Access dates (for comparison): Deluxe digital starts early on September 4, 2026; Standard digital Early Access begins September 8, 2026.  
2. Full digital launch and physical release both occur on October 6, 2026 — physical editions arrive on that date for PS5 and Xbox Series X.  
3. For full digital-edition feature and early-access differences see [Digital editions]({{page:standard-vs-deluxe-upgrade}}).

What comes on the disc
- The physical editions include the full game on disc. The disc provides the playable game at retail launch (October 6, 2026).  
- Any Advance Access items included with the physical edition are delivered as in‑game unlocks or codes tied to that purchase (see Advance Access section below).

Clown and Samhain cosmetics — what "Advance Access" means
- The Clown Michael skin is included as Advance Access in the Standard physical edition.  
- The Limited Collector’s Edition adds Samhain Michael as Advance Access in addition to the Clown skin.  
- "Advance Access" denotes early access to those cosmetics for purchasers of the listed edition; it does not mean they are permanently locked to physical buyers.

Physical Advance Access vs exclusivity
- Official messaging states the Advance Access cosmetics included with physical editions can also be earned later through in‑game challenges. In other words, Advance Access is early availability, not a permanent physical-only exclusive.  
- For details on in‑game progression and how cosmetics are unlocked through play, see [Progression & Perks]({{page:progression-perks}}). For more on Michael Myers skins specifically, see [Michael Myers skins]({{page:michael-myers-skins}}).

Does the physical edition include Phantom Michael?
- No. Phantom Michael is a Digital Pre‑order Exclusive. Physical editions and physical preorders do not grant Phantom Michael.  
- According to the official pre-order wording, Phantom is only available via digital preorders and is not offered for sale after launch (digital preorder exclusive).

Common mistakes to avoid
1. Assuming Advance Access = permanent exclusive — Advance Access items can be earned later via challenges.  
2. Expecting Phantom Michael with physical purchase — Phantom is strictly a digital pre-order exclusive.  
3. Confusing platform availability — the physical release is confirmed for PS5 and Xbox Series X only.

FAQ
Q: When is the physical release?  
A: October 6, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X. (Digital Early Access dates differ; see above and [Digital editions]({{page:standard-vs-deluxe-upgrade}}).)

Q: Can I still get Phantom Michael if I miss the digital pre-order?  
A: Phantom Michael is listed as a Digital Pre‑order Exclusive and is not included with physical editions; the official guidance indicates it’s unavailable after launch as a preorder exclusive.

Q: Can I unlock the Clown or Samhain skins without buying a physical edition?  
A: Yes — the Advance Access versions are early unlocks for physical buyers, but the same cosmetics can be earned later through in‑game challenges. See [Progression & Perks]({{page:progression-perks}}) for unlocking details.

Q: Is the Limited Collector’s Edition guaranteed to include everything shown?  
A: The Collector’s contents listed by the publisher include the items named (Shape Jump Variant Action Figure, lenticular art, poster, listed Advance Access items) plus “more surprises” per the official product copy. Do not assume additional unlisted items beyond the official wording.
