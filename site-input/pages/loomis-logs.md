# Halloween The Game Loomis Log locations

Quick answer
Story Mode contains 14 recording collectibles in total: 11 numbered Loomis Logs and 3 other recordings. This page lists each of the 14 story recordings by chapter and gives the exact in‑scene location where the collectible appears. Note the Chapter 4/5 access caveats and the Chapter 5 marker bug (details below) before hunting.

Key facts (exact tokens)
- 14 story recordings  
- 11 numbered Loomis Logs + 3 other recordings  
- Chapter 4 laundry-room timing/access risk  
- Chapter 5 Loomis Log #11 marker bug: glow may remain on the nightstand while the collectible is near the window

How to use this list (quick workflow)
1. Open the chapter you’re playing and check only the locations listed for that chapter.  
2. If a collectible is missing where listed, continue the story (some houses only become accessible later).  
3. For Chapter 4 detached laundry and any Chapter 5 house items, be prepared to return later if access is blocked or the scene changes.

Common mistakes
- Mislabeling the three non-numbered recordings as Loomis Logs. The three other recordings are Sheriff Brackett's Recording, Laurie's Recording, and Nurse Chambers Recording — list them separately from numbered Loomis Logs.  
- Assuming every collectible will appear on first visit to a house (see: Some Chapter 4/5 recordings require later story access to the relevant house).  
- Expecting the interaction marker to always be reliable. In particular, Chapter 5 Loomis Log #11 marker bug: glow may remain on the nightstand while the collectible is near the window — the visual cue can be misleading.  
- Relying on a complete set of screenshots: Functional screenshots for each collectible are incomplete; do not assume media coverage confirms every spawn.
