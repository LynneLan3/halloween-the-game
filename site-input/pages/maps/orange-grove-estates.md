# Orange Grove Estates — Map Guide

**Confirmed:** Orange Grove Estates is IllFonic's third revealed multiplayer map—a larger upscale suburban neighborhood with multi-story houses and more open sightlines than Haddonfield Heights.

## Known landmarks and layout

Official materials highlight:

- **Wallace house** and **Doyle house**
- **Multi-story homes** with garages and balconies
- **Fences, hedges, and chokepoints** between properties
- A **neighborhood park** with a **playground** and **basketball court**
- **Vehicles and bikes** scattered through the streets

IllFonic notes more entrances, windows, and verticality than the modest single-story homes on Haddonfield Heights ({{page:maps-haddonfield-heights}}).

## Gameplay characteristics (official)

Orange Grove Estates is positioned as a map where:

- Civilians must manage **multiple entrances** and longer lines of sight
- **Open spaces** (park, courts, streets) create different stalking and escape tension than Town Center's storefront interiors ({{page:maps-haddonfield-town-center}})
- Environmental interactables and varied routing support the randomized escape and item systems described in the multiplayer overview—see {{page:multiplayer-how-it-works}}
