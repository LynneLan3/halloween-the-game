# Halloween: The Game matchmaking queue — Roles, Tokens & Private Matches

Quick answer
- Online matches are 1 Michael vs 4 Civilians (up to 5 players total).
- Press coverage of IllFonic’s matchmaking overview reports role-based queues: separate Killer and Civilian queues plus a flexible “No Preference” option.
- Private matches with friends are supported; press reporting says hosts can set roles or randomize them. IllFonic’s official Progression & Customization overview also lists private matches against AI as a supported play option.
- Press reporting (Playfront) describes a “Killer Token” priority tied to the Michael queue: tokens are reported to be earned by playing/completing matches, can enter the Michael priority pool, and are not consumed until the player actually enters a match as Michael. Treat those token mechanics as secondary reporting rather than an official IllFonic policy.
- Official Patch 1.0.1 notes: cross-play party members now follow their leader into a matchmade lobby instead of being kicked/dropped; the Public Match pre-match countdown is longer when the lobby is not full.
- Unconfirmed by official materials: whether public matchmaking uses AI backfill and whether private lobbies automatically fill empty slots with bots.

Role queues (press-reported)
- Multiple press outlets covering IllFonic’s matchmaking overview report a role-based system: distinct Killer and Civilian queues plus a No Preference option that can match players into either role. Treat these descriptions as press reporting of the developer’s overview rather than an IllFonic FAQ.
- The reporting frames this as a player-choice system allowing queuing specifically as Michael or specifically as a Civilian, or searching without specifying a role.

Killer Tokens (secondary reporting)
- Secondary reporting (Playfront) describes a Killer Token priority mechanic for access to the Michael queue. The reporting states:
  - Tokens are earned by playing/completing matches.
  - Tokens can place you into the Michael priority pool.
  - Tokens are not consumed until you enter a match as Michael.
- These points are reported by secondary outlets; they are not presented here as confirmed IllFonic policy or a complete token-economy FAQ.

Private matches and AI
- Press coverage of IllFonic’s matchmaking overview confirms private matches with friends and reports hosts can elect specific roles or randomize role assignment for those private games.
- IllFonic’s official Progression & Customization overview explicitly lists private matches against AI as a play option.
- Official and press materials do not confirm whether private lobbies automatically fill empty slots with bots.

What is still unknown (officially)
- Whether public matchmaking will use AI backfill for missing players.
- Whether private lobbies will automatically fill empty slots with bots.
- Token economy specifics such as earn rates, caps, or exact numbers (these were not provided in cited reporting).
- Any ranked vs casual queue structure or live wait-time guarantees.

Patch 1.0.1 (official patch notes)
- From the official Patch 1.0.1 notes: cross-play party members now follow their leader into a matchmade lobby instead of being kicked or dropped.
- From the same official notes: the Public Match pre-match countdown is longer when the lobby is not full.
- These points are taken from the official 1.0.1 patch notes and are kept distinct from the matchmaking overview described by press coverage.

Common mistakes to avoid
- Assuming public matchmaking currently uses AI backfill — this has not been confirmed by official materials.
- Assuming private lobbies auto-fill with bots — private matches against AI are an option, but automatic bot-fill of empty private slots is unannounced.
- Treating Playfront or other secondary coverage as an IllFonic FAQ — treat token mechanics reported by press as secondary reporting unless IllFonic confirms them.

What happens next
- Use IllFonic’s official Progression & Customization and multiplayer overviews for confirmed features; consult the publisher/developer channels and official patch notes for any changes to matchmaking behavior or token mechanics.
