### Secondary reporting (Playfront) — label clearly, do not over-claim

Playfront reports IllFonic uses role-based matchmaking with separate Killer / Civilian queues, plus a flexible no-preference option, and a **Killer Token** priority system:

- Tokens earned by completing matches
- Redeemed before search for preferential Killer queue access
- Tokens deducted only when a match actually starts as Michael
- Lobby disconnects reportedly do not spend tokens
