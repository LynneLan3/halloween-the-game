## Confirmed Skin Set (explain these only)

### Base Michael

- Default / base Michael Myers appearance available through normal play ownership of Michael.

### Phantom Michael

- **Digital Pre-order Exclusive**
- Unavailable for purchase after launch
- Physical preorder / physical editions do **not** grant Phantom

### Inmate Michael

- Included with **Digital Deluxe**
- Official **Deluxe Upgrade at launch** includes Inmate
- Not the same as Phantom exclusivity

### Clown Michael

- Physical edition **Advance Access** cosmetic (Physical Standard and Collector’s)
- Also earnable later via **in-game challenges**
- Do **not** call it a permanent physical exclusive

### Samhain Michael

- Limited Collector’s Edition **Advance Access** cosmetic
- Also earnable later via **in-game challenges**
- Do **not** call it a permanent physical exclusive
