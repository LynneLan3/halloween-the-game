## Official Facts

### Multiplayer role

- 1 Michael Myers vs 4 Civilians
- Michael hunts/kills residents and Special Targets
- Civilians/police can knock Michael down
- Michael cannot simply be killed
- Sufficient resistance can detain him and return him to Smith’s Grove

### Sep 2 Progression & Customization update — loadout model

Michael loadouts have:

- **2 starting traits:** Killer Sense, Shape Jump
- players then select **3 Michael abilities**
- official examples of selectable abilities include: Reality Tear, Blackout, Detection Pulse
- progression unlocks new Michael abilities, starting weapons, executions, skins/tints

### Important correction

Do **not** present Killer Sense / Stalk / Shape Jump / Blackout as the same kind of “core ability” list.

- Killer Sense and Shape Jump are the Sep 2–defined **starting traits**
- Players then choose **3 selectable abilities**
- Official examples include Reality Tear, Blackout, Detection Pulse — these are examples only, not a complete ability roster
- **Stalk** remains an officially described Michael gameplay mechanic, but it is **not** listed as one of the two Sep 2 starting traits

### Single-player

- Michael is the playable protagonist
- six story chapters
- single-player also teaches/prepares players for Michael gameplay
