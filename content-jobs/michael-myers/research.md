## Official Facts

### Multiplayer role

- 1 Michael Myers vs 4 Civilians
- Michael hunts/kills residents and Special Targets
- Civilians/police can knock Michael down
- Michael cannot simply be killed
- Sufficient resistance can detain him and return him to Smith’s Grove

### Sep 2 Progression & Customization update — loadout model

Michael loadouts have:

- **2 starting traits:** Killer Sense, Shape Jump
- players then select **3 Michael abilities**
- official examples of selectable abilities include: Reality Tear, Blackout, Detection Pulse
- progression unlocks new Michael abilities, starting weapons, executions, skins/tints

### Important correction

Do **not** present Killer Sense / Stalk / Shape Jump / Blackout as the same kind of “core ability” list.

- Killer Sense and Shape Jump are the Sep 2–defined **starting traits**
- Players then choose **3 selectable abilities**
- Official examples include Reality Tear, Blackout, Detection Pulse — these are examples only, not a complete ability roster
- **Stalk** remains an officially described Michael gameplay mechanic, but it is **not** listed as one of the two Sep 2 starting traits

### Single-player

- Michael is the playable protagonist
- six story chapters
- single-player also teaches/prepares players for Michael gameplay

### Evil Presence — evidence-bounded EA findings

#### Confirmed in-game / official

- Official progression material says Killer progression unlocks new Michael abilities, weapons, executions, and cosmetics. It does not publish Evil Presence's numeric unlock level.
- Official loadout material confirms two starting traits plus three selectable Michael abilities. Evil Presence should be described as an observed selectable Michael ability only where the EA gameplay evidence supports it.

#### Observed in EA gameplay

- [The BEST Ability On Myers In Halloween The Game (Evil Presence)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUohGl2xF7c) — Middi, published Sep 6, 2026. The video identifies Evil Presence, describes a concentrated area/aura around Michael, and repeatedly demonstrates nearby Civilians being **staggered**, giving Michael an opening to close distance or interrupt an escape. The player references cooldown and needing blood first, but gives no reliable numeric values.
- [MICHAEL MYERS' NEW ABILITY | Halloween: The Game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z6SAsHMCJ_c) — Xander noob, published Sep 6, 2026. Independent EA loadout gameplay; the clearly described knife clip briefly stuns a target, but the video does not provide enough clean named evidence to attribute that effect to Evil Presence. Use only as secondary context, not as direct Evil Presence proof.

#### Still unverified

- Exact in-game tooltip/loadout wording, numeric Killer Level unlock, cooldown, duration, range, damage, probability, and any health/strength boost.
- Whether the effect formally requires an active chase state. The footage shows use during pursuit/loops and near escapes, but does not establish a state requirement.
- Whether “stagger” is the same mechanic as a guaranteed Civilian stumble/trip. Do not publish trip as confirmed from this evidence.
- Any direct requirement for Stalk Tier 3, or a guaranteed execution chain. The footage discusses Stalk/finishers separately but does not prove an Evil Presence → Tier 3 → execution rule.
- Shape Mines and Bloodthirst numeric/effect details; do not fill these gaps from one player's commentary.

### Ability review boundary

- Killer Sense: official prey-awareness mechanic.
- Shape Jump: official shadow movement; direct darkness and line-of-sight limitations are documented by the developer.
- Shape Dash: official burst movement from Shape Jump context.
- Blackout: official light/power manipulation and temporary blackout context.
- Reality Tear and Detection Pulse: officially named selectable examples; exact effects remain unverified here.
- Shadow Strike: one EA video shows a knife ability briefly stunning a target, but the clean named tooltip/mapping is not confirmed.
- Evil Presence: observed stagger/aura effect only, with the unknowns above preserved.
- Shape Mines, Bloodthirst, exact Stalk-tier rules: remain UNKNOWN unless a primary or clearly attributable gameplay frame proves more.
