## Official Facts

From IllFonic “The Locations of Halloween: The Game” (Sep 1, 2026) and Launch and Early Access post (Sep 3, 2026):

### Launch map set

Halloween: The Game launches with **4 unique multiplayer maps**:

1. Haddonfield Town Center
2. Haddonfield Heights
3. Orange Grove Estates
4. **East Haddonfield**

### East Haddonfield setting

- On the outskirts of town; Michael enters East Haddonfield following escape from Smith’s Grove Sanitarium
- Rural agricultural hub with plenty of farmland
- Headquarters for **Midwest Feed Company**
- Attracts unsavory individuals such as the biker gang **Castle Hill Rooks**
- Described as a rustic change of scenery compared with Orange Grove Estates and Haddonfield Town Center

### Confirmed landmarks / named locations

- **The Rabbit in Red Lounge** — rowdy, leather-clad biker bar
- **Phelps Garage** — automotive services / gas on the outskirts
- **Midwest Feed Company** — economic anchor / employer for many area residents
