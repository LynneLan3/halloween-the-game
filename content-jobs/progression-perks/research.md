## Official Facts

From IllFonic Progression & Customization overview (Sep 2, 2026):

### Play options that earn progression

- Singleplayer story mode
- Private matches against AI
- Online 1v4 matchmaking

### Four Player Progression Levels

Players earn XP applied to four categories:

1. **Profile Level** — XP regardless of role
2. **Killer Level** — earned playing as Michael
3. **Civilian Level** — tied to each playable Civilian character
4. **Weapon Level** — earned by dispatching victims with specific equipable Michael weapons

Growing progression levels unlocks:

- additional playable Civilian characters
- new Michael abilities
- supplemental Starting Items for Civilians
- cosmetic rewards
- new tiers of challenges
- executions
- and more

Players can **prestige** upon reaching the max progression level in any of these four categories.

### Challenges

- **Passive Challenges** — categories for Killer and Civilian; tracked regardless of character
- **Progressive Challenges** — tied to specific Civilian characters, Michael Skins, and weapon types; tackled in succession
- **Singleplayer Challenges** — optional challenges for all six story chapters; unlock alternate outfits / unique cosmetics, including a legendary look for completing every singleplayer challenge
- IllFonic states the game is packed with **100s of challenges**

### Michael customization / loadout

- Two starting traits: **Killer Sense** and **Shape Jump**
- Choose **three** distinct Michael abilities (examples: Reality Tear, Blackout, Detection Pulse, and more)
- Killer Skins and Tints
- Unlockable starting weapons with earnable tints
- Executions

### Civilian customization / loadout

- Unique stats and signature traits per Civilian
- Outfits with multiple tints, hair/headwear, eyewear, makeup/blemishes
- Unlockable starting items
- **Civilian Perk Decks**

### Civilian stats (names only — no fabricated numbers)

Officially named stats:

- Athleticism
- Personality
- Resourcefulness
- Capability

Do not invent numeric values for these stats.

### Civilian Perks

- Completing matches supplies **Perk Points**
- Perk Points roll for **Perk Cards** that provide gameplay boosts
- Spending more Perk Points can increase odds of higher-rarity cards
- Perk Points can temporarily upgrade owned cards for a set number of matches
- Players construct **Perk Decks** equipped to Civilian Loadouts
- Randomized in-game Perk Card rolls help vary matches

### Profile Cards

- Profile Icons, Backgrounds, and Titles earned via progression/challenges
- Used to build Profile Cards for online multiplayer flair
