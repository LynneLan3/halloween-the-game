## Confirmed Official Facts

### Timing & platforms

- Physical release date: **October 6, 2026**
- Platforms for physical: **PS5** and **Xbox Series X**
- Digital Early Access starts earlier (September 4 / September 8, 2026 by edition). Physical launches on the full release day.

### Pricing

- Physical Standard Edition: **$39.99**
- Limited Collector’s Edition: **$149.99**

### Disc

- The game is **on disc** for physical editions.

### Physical Standard Edition contents

- Halloween: The Game (on disc)
- Michael Myers Clown Skin Advance Access
- 2 Civilian cosmetics Advance Access
- Poster

### Limited Collector’s Edition contents

- Halloween: The Game (on disc)
- Michael Myers Clown Skin Advance Access
- Michael Myers Samhain Skin Advance Access
- 4 Civilian cosmetics Advance Access
- Poster
- Shape Jump Variant Action Figure
- Lenticular art
- More surprises (official wording — do not invent a full unlisted inventory)

### Advance Access vs exclusivity (critical)

Official FAQ: physical “Advance Access” cosmetics are **not** permanent physical-only locks. They can **also be earned later via in-game challenges**.

### Phantom Michael (critical)

- **Phantom Michael** is a **Digital Pre-order Exclusive**.
- Physical preorder / physical editions do **NOT** grant Phantom Michael.
- After launch, Phantom is unavailable for purchase (digital preorder exclusive rule).

### Related digital context (for timing comparison only)

- Digital Early Access: Deluxe Sep 4, 2026 / Standard Sep 8, 2026
- Full digital launch: Oct 6, 2026
- Point readers to {{page:standard-vs-deluxe-upgrade}} and {{page:michael-myers-skins}} for digital edition/skin detail.
