### Safe player guidance (procedural, not invented facts)

Players should:

1. Check their storefront library / product page (Steam, Epic, PlayStation, Xbox) for a Preload / Download button or download status.
2. Distinguish **download/preload** from **play unlock** — Early Access play starts Sep 4, 9 AM PT for eligible Deluxe buyers; full launch play starts Sep 8, 9 AM PT.
3. Allow downloads/updates to finish before the unlock time.
4. Verify the correct storefront account holds the Deluxe entitlement if chasing Early Access.
