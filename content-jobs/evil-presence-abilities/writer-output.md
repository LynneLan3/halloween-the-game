# Michael Myers Abilities — Killer Sense, Stalk, Shape Jump & Blackouts

Quick answer
Evil Presence is an unlockable Michael ability observed in Early Access that creates a short-range aura around Michael which visibly staggers or interrupts nearby Civilians’ movement, creating an opening for Michael to close distance or interrupt an escape. Community reports say it can sometimes make Civilians trip/fall and that players have used it in a Tier 3 Stalk → chase → Evil Presence → execution sequence, but those outcomes are community‑reported and not proven. Official sources do not publish Evil Presence’s exact unlock level, cooldown, range, duration, damage, probability, or resource cost—those remain launch verification pending.

Confirmed facts
- Evil Presence is an unlockable Michael ability seen in Early Access multiplayer (it occupies a selectable Michael ability slot; it is not one of Michael’s two starting traits).
- Observed EA gameplay shows Evil Presence as a short-range area/aura around Michael that visibly staggers or interrupts nearby Civilians’ movement.
- In the successful runs reviewed, the observed stagger/interruption created an opening for Michael to close distance or interrupt an escape attempt.
- Some players report that Evil Presence can make Civilians trip or fall; this is community‑reported and not proven as a guaranteed trip mechanic.
- Some players describe using Evil Presence after a Tier 3 Stalk during a chase that led to an execution (Tier 3 Stalk → chase → Evil Presence → execution); that sequence is community‑reported and not a demonstrated hard requirement or guarantee.
- Official materials do not publish Evil Presence’s exact Killer Level, cooldown, range, duration, damage, probability, blood/resource cost, formal Chase State requirement, Stalk Tier 3 requirement, or Bloodthirst interaction—treat those fields as launch verification pending.
- Michael loadouts have 2 starting traits (Killer Sense + Shape Jump); players separately choose 3 Michael abilities, and Killer progression unlocks new Michael abilities.
- Official multiplayer overview documents Michael’s core kit (Killer Sense, Stalk, Shape Jump and Shape Dash, blackouts/light manipulation, and melee combat with civilian resistance).
- Secondary video context (Xander noob footage) shows short stun/stagger gameplay near Michael but cannot cleanly prove that effect is Evil Presence; use it only as secondary context, not core proof.

FAQ (short)
Q — Does Evil Presence require Stalk Tier 3?  
A — Some players describe a Tier 3 Stalk → chase → Evil Presence → execution sequence in community reports, but this is not confirmed as a requirement. Treat that sequence as community‑reported, not a hard requirement.

Q — Does Evil Presence guarantee an execution?  
A — No guaranteed execution has been published or shown in official materials. Observed runs and community reports show it can be used in sequences that end in an execution, but there is no proof that Evil Presence by itself guarantees an execution.

Q — How do you unlock Evil Presence?  
A — Evil Presence is observed as an unlockable Michael ability chosen from the 3 selectable Michael ability slots; official progression documentation confirms Michael unlocks new abilities through Killer progression but does not publish the exact Killer Level required for Evil Presence. The exact unlock level and other numeric unlock conditions are launch verification pending.

See the confirmed sections below for details.

**Confirmed:** IllFonic's multiplayer gameplay overview documents Michael Myers' core ability set for Halloween: The Game. This page lists what is officially stated—not speculative meta.

## Killer Sense

**Confirmed:**

- First-person **awareness mode**
- Helps Michael **detect nearby prey**

Killer Sense is Michael's information tool for closing on Civilians who are searching, escorting residents, or preparing escape options.

## Stalk

**Confirmed:**

- Builds while Michael **focuses on targets**
- Improves **identification and tracking**
- Unlocks **increasingly strong execution opportunities** as Stalk progresses

IllFonic positions Stalk as the escalation mechanic that rewards sustained pressure on a target.

## Shape Jump

**Confirmed:**

- Michael **enters shadows** and moves **unseen**
- Increases **mobility** while active
- Can only **enter or exit** while hidden in **darkness** or **outside resident line of sight**

Shape Jump is the foundation for ambush play and map rotation when lights are out.

## Shape Dash

**Confirmed:**

- A **traversal option available from Shape Jump**
- Delivers a **forward burst / lunge**

Use Shape Dash to close distance after setting up darkness or breaking line of sight.

## Blackout / light manipulation

**Confirmed:**

- Michael can **shut off lights**, **cut power**, and **trigger temporary blackouts**
- Darkness **enables Shape Jump opportunities**

This ties Michael's map control directly to mobility—especially on maps with varied interiors like Haddonfield Town Center ({{page:maps-haddonfield-town-center}}).

## Combat and resistance

**Confirmed:**

- Michael has **melee combat** options
- Civilians **can fight back**
- Michael can be **knocked down / resisted**
- Michael **cannot simply be killed**
- Strong enough **community / police resistance** can lead to **detention** and return to **Smith's Grove**

Exact combat numbers and detention triggers are **launch verification pending**.
