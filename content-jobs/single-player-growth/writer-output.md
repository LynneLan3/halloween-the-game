# Halloween: The Game Single Player — Story Mode & Chapters

Quick answer
Story Mode is a single-player narrative containing a Prologue plus five numbered chapters (Prologue + Chapters 1–5). In the successful runs reviewed, the campaign proceeds with the Prologue first and then Chapters 1–5 in that named order. Observed chapter progressions from reviewed walkthroughs are summarized below (each is an observed sequence from those runs, not a guaranteed requirement). Optional challenges and collectibles are replay content tied to the six playable chapters (the Prologue and Chapters 1–5). Current community reports list possible save/reset, challenge/log registration, relock/loop, and Chapter 4 AI/timing problems during Early Access; those reports are not confirmed as universal and do not establish a guaranteed workaround.

- Observed campaign order (in the successful runs reviewed): Prologue → Chapter 1 → Chapter 2 → Chapter 3 → Chapter 4 → Chapter 5.

Does Halloween: The Game have single player?
Yes. Official materials describe a narrative Story Mode played from Michael Myers’ perspective. Story Mode:
- Reimagines events surrounding the 1978 film.
- Begins around Michael’s escape from Smith’s Grove.
- Is narrated by Dr. Samuel Loomis.
- Uses familiar and new locations and follows Michael toward Laurie Strode.
This page focuses only on confirmed chapter structure and observed sequences; other chapter-level details remain under verification.

How chapters work (confirmed)
- Story Mode contains a Prologue and five numbered chapters.
- The observed campaign order in the reviewed runs is Prologue, then Chapters 1–5 in the named order above.
- Each chapter contains fixed core objectives while gameplay execution in those objectives remains sandbox-driven across runs.
- Multiple difficulty options are available; exact difficulty modifiers and full objective lists require further verification.
