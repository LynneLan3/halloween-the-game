## Official Verified Facts

- Perk Cards are a Civilian customization system.
- Completing matches supplies Perk Points.
- Perk Points roll randomized Perk Cards that provide gameplay boosts.
- Spending more points improves the odds of a higher-rarity result; it is not a guarantee.
- Perk Points can temporarily upgrade an owned card for a set number of matches.
- Owned cards can be assembled into Perk Decks and equipped to Civilian Loadouts.
- In-match card selection is randomized, so the same loadout does not produce an identical match every time.

## Launch-Build Corroborated Mechanics

Label these as live-launch-build behavior, not developer-published rules.

- A deck can contain up to 15 cards.
- The 15 cards are not all active at match start.
- At a progression or XP threshold, players are offered three cards and choose one.
- Dead or escaped players can provide extra perks to living teammates through support minigames.
- If a deck has fewer than 15 selected cards, random filler may appear; this is corroborated player reporting and not guaranteed across patches.
- Multiple saved decks exist; one secondary UI source reports up to six saved deck presets. Do not confuse saved presets with cards per deck.

## Traits vs Perk Cards

- Civilian signature Traits and rollable Perk Cards are separate systems.
- Some names or effects overlap. The known overlapping names are: Brimstone Fuse, Aura Of Awareness, Sixth Sense, Guardian Shadow, Repair Expert, Trespasser, Ghost, Barricade, Fade To Black, Lethal Pitch, Exit Strategy, Convincing Plea, Knockout, and Smooth Talker.
- Explain the distinction clearly. Do not infer that same-name effects have identical scaling, that Trait tier equals Perk Card tier, or that either system stacks with the other.

## Economy, Rarity, and Scrap Boundaries

- Verified: spending more Perk Points only improves higher-rarity odds; it does not guarantee a high-rarity roll.
- Verified: temporary upgrades last a set number of matches. Exact duration is unknown.
- Strong secondary evidence shows a Scrap action and a Perk Point return. The exact refund formula is unknown.
- Do not publish a fixed roll-price table: 100 / 500 / 1000 / 2500 reports conflict and do not establish one complete current ladder.
