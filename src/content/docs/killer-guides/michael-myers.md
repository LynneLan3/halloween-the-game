# Michael Myers Abilities in Halloween: The Game — Evil Presence, Stalk & Unlocks

## Quick answer

- What Evil Presence does: Early EA gameplay shows Evil Presence as a short-range aura around Michael that can stagger nearby Civilians, creating an opening for Michael to close distance or interrupt an escape. This description is based on recorded play and is labeled as observed, not fully documented.
- How you unlock/use it: Evil Presence appears as a selectable Michael ability that is obtained through progression. The exact Killer Level unlock, range, duration, cooldown, and any formal activation requirements are UNKNOWN from available official material and observed footage.
- How it relates to Stalk/executions: Footage demonstrates Evil Presence used during pursuit/pressure situations, but it does not prove Evil Presence is a Stalk Tier‑3 requirement or that it guarantees execution chains. Stalk remains an official Michael mechanic, but it is not one of the two Sep 2 starting traits.

## Confirmed in‑game / official (what the developer has published)

- Multiplayer format: matches are 1 Michael Myers vs 4 Civilians.
- Michael hunts and kills residents and Special Targets.
- Civilians and responding police can knock Michael down.
- Michael cannot simply be killed like a Civilian; sufficient resistance can [detain him](/how-to-arrest-michael-myers/) and return him to Smith’s Grove (ending his threat for that match).
- Sep 2 loadout model: Michael loadouts start with two fixed starting traits — Killer Sense and Shape Jump — and players then select three Michael abilities for that loadout.
- Official examples of selectable abilities include Reality Tear, Blackout, and Detection Pulse (these are examples only, not a complete roster).
- Progression unlocks new Michael abilities, starting weapons, executions, and skins/tints according to the developer overview.
- Single‑player: Michael is the playable protagonist in a six‑chapter story designed also to prepare players for multiplayer Michael gameplay.
- Stalk is an officially described Michael gameplay mechanic but is not one of the two Sep 2 starting traits.

For per-ability detail, see [Michael abilities](/michael-myers/abilities/).
