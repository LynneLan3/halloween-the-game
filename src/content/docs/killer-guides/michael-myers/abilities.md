Start Michael with his two starting traits (Killer Sense and Shape Jump) and use the supported launch starter build: Reality Tear + Detection Pulse + Blackout. Why this starter works (short)
- Detection Pulse: gives information about occupied buildings and hiding spots so you can choose where to press an encounter.
- Blackout: creates darkness and disables lights/electronics, which directly supports Shape Jump approaches.
- Reality Tear: provides traversal/chase options that can bypass normal Shape Jump limitations and create surprise angles or cutoffs.

How to use the loadout — step-by-step
Follow these steps in match order to test the synergy and learn practical timing.

1. At round start, use Killer Sense to pick a likely target area (it gives directional awareness of nearby activity).
2. Use Detection Pulse to check which buildings are occupied or which areas have clustered Residents. Treat it as an information tool, not a damage ability.
3. Move to the detected area and decide whether you can safely cut power. If so, trigger Blackout to darken the area and remove light/electronics.
4. While the area is dark, use Shape Jump approaches (enter/exiting shadows) to reposition unseen or close distance from cover. Blackout + Shape Jump is the core stealth/approach synergy.
5. Use Reality Tear when you need a nonstandard route — to cross open ground, create an unexpected angle, or cut off flight paths. Use it to maintain pressure in a chase or reach positions Shape Jump alone can’t.
6. Repeat Detection Pulse periodically to refresh where Residents are hiding or to find split-up targets.
7. If targets spread, use Detection Pulse to re-locate them and Reality Tear to intercept exits rather than merely chasing predictable paths.

Notes on expectations and limits
- Detection Pulse and Blackout are tactical tools to shape engagements; neither is a guaranteed kill mechanic by itself.
- Reality Tear adds traversal flexibility; it helps but does not guarantee a successful catch. Learn routes in live matches.
- Avoid overcommitting to a single Detection Pulse reading — Residents can bait you into exposed areas.
