Why this starter works (short)
- Detection Pulse: an information tool for locating players or occupied buildings, so you can prioritize where to hunt or which houses to contest.
- Blackout: removes lights/electronics and creates darkness that synergizes with Shape Jump, letting you enter/exit shadowed approaches more safely.
- Reality Tear: expands traversal and chase options beyond ordinary Shape Jump constraints, opening alternate routes or surprise approaches that standard Shape Jump may not allow.

How to use the loadout — step-by-step
(Use this order in a match to put players under pressure and test practical synergy.)

1. Begin the round with Killer Sense to get directional awareness of nearby activity. Use it to pick a target area to test with Detection Pulse.
2. Use Detection Pulse to confirm occupied buildings or clusters of Civilians. Treat it strictly as an information tool — it helps you decide where to Blackout or force a fight.
3. Move toward the detected area. If you can cut power safely, trigger Blackout to create darkness and remove lights/electronics in the area.
4. Immediately apply Shape Jump options while the area is dark to reposition unseen or close distance from cover. Blackout + Shape Jump is the primary stealth/approach synergy of this build.
5. Use Reality Tear to bypass normal Shape Jump constraints when you need a nonstandard route (example: crossing an open area or creating a surprise angle). Reality Tear is best used to maintain pressure in a chase or to reach targets Shape Jump alone would struggle to access.
6. Repeat Detection Pulse periodically to refresh information on occupied buildings and to chase down fleeing Civilians who split up.
7. Adjust: if a target group spreads, use Detection Pulse to re-locate them and use Reality Tear to cut off exits rather than chasing around predictable paths.

Notes on practical timing and expectations
- Do not assume Detection Pulse or Blackout directly damages or forces down a Civilian — treat them as tactical tools to shape encounters.
- Reality Tear gives traversal flexibility; it can make a chase less predictable but is not a guaranteed instant catch. Test routes in live matches to learn viable angles.
- Avoid overcommitting on a bad call: Detection Pulse narrows search, but civilians can bait you into exposed areas.

Evil Presence — observed behavior, evidence, and limits
Evil Presence is an unlockable Michael ability observed in Early Access. It occupies a selectable Michael ability slot — it is not one of Michael’s two official starting traits (Killer Sense + Shape Jump).

Observed EA gameplay shows Evil Presence as a short-range area/aura around Michael. Nearby Civilians can be visibly staggered or have their movement interrupted. In the reviewed successful runs, that stagger/interruption created an opening for Michael to close distance or interrupt an escape.
