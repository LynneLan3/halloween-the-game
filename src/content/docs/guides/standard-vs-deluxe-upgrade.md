# Halloween: The Game — Standard vs Digital Deluxe: Which Edition Should I Buy?

Quick answer

- Standard is the base game at $39.99. Digital Deluxe is $59.99 — exactly $20 more — and adds the Inmate Michael Myers skin plus the Richard and Alexis civilians and other Deluxe/preorder extras.
- Digital Deluxe preorders include Advance Access beginning Friday, September 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT ([Advance Access timing](/early-access-release-time/)). Buying a Deluxe upgrade after launch does not have an announced Advance Access policy.
- You can upgrade a Standard purchase to Digital Deluxe at launch using the Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU, but the upgrade price per storefront is unannounced.
- Important irreversible consequence: the Phantom Michael Myers skin is a preorder-only item and cannot be recovered later by purchasing the Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade.

## Confirmed

- Editions and prices: Standard — **$39.99 USD**; Digital Deluxe — **$59.99 USD** (official FAQ and storefronts). That is a $20 difference between editions.
- Digital Deluxe confirmed contents: base game, **Inmate Michael Myers skin**, exclusive civilians **Richard** and **Alexis**, comic and other preorder extras — [characters guide](/characters/).
- Phantom Michael Myers skin: confirmed digital pre-order exclusive; official materials describe it as unavailable after the preorder period. The Deluxe Upgrade at launch does **not** restore Phantom.
- Standard owners can buy the Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade at launch (storefront sku exists); exact upgrade price is not announced.
- Advance Access: Digital Deluxe preorder Advance Access begins Friday, September 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. The Standard listing does not include that Advance Access bullet.
- Single-player / offline bots: official product copy lists a single-player story mode and offline play against bots as separate from online multiplayer.

## Which edition should you buy — conditional recommendation

- If you only want the full base game and plan to play on or after the full release date (Sep 8) → Buy Standard ($39.99).
- If you want early access on Sep 4 and the Deluxe cosmetic/character extras → Preorder Digital Deluxe ($59.99) before the preorder cutoff.
- If you're unsure now but may want Deluxe content later → Buy Standard now; you can purchase the Deluxe Edition Upgrade at launch. Keep in mind the upgrade price per storefront is unannounced and Advance Access from Sep 4 may not be granted to users who upgrade after preorder.
- If your decision hinges on the Phantom Michael Myers skin → preorder (whichever SKU offers Phantom while the preorder window is active). The Phantom skin is preorder-only and will not be added later by buying the Deluxe Upgrade.

## Upgrade details — can you upgrade later?

1. Standard owners can purchase a Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade at launch (storefront listings and IllFonic FAQ confirm an upgrade SKU exists).
2. The Deluxe Upgrade price is not announced — do not assume it will equal the $20 difference shown between edition MSRP.
3. The Deluxe Upgrade grants the Deluxe civilians and the Inmate Michael Myers skin, but the upgrade does **not** restore the Phantom Michael Myers preorder skin.

## Early access (Advance Access) — what we know

- Advance Access tied to Digital Deluxe preorders begins Friday, September 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. See [Advance Access timing](/early-access-release-time/) for the exact published time.
- Official materials confirm Advance Access for Digital Deluxe preorders. IllFonic has not announced whether a post-purchase Deluxe Upgrade or a Deluxe purchase without a preorder will grant the same Sep 4 Advance Access — that remains unannounced.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade at launch will give you the Phantom skin. It will not — Phantom is preorder-only and not recoverable via the upgrade.
- Assuming Advance Access is unlocked by any post-launch Deluxe purchase. Advance Access is confirmed for Digital Deluxe preorders; post-preorder upgrade/access policies are not announced.
- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade price is the same everywhere or already known. The upgrade price per storefront is unannounced.

## What happens next

- If you want Sep 4 Advance Access or the Phantom preorder item: preorder Digital Deluxe before the preorder window closes.
- If you buy Standard now and later decide you want Deluxe content: wait for the launch Deluxe Upgrade SKU; expect to pay an additional amount (storefront price unannounced) to add Deluxe content, but know the Phantom preorder item will remain unavailable if you did not preorder it.
