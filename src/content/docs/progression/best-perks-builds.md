## Recommended builds (Patch 1.0.1) — how to use them

Use these as role-focused starting decks. Each block lists the recommended cards, why they fit the role given Patch 1.0.1 changes, and quick on-field priorities. The builds are recommendations, not an official ranking.

### Starter / General

- Unwinded, Adrenaline, Sixth Sense (swap Slippery for Unwinded if grabs are your main loss condition).

Why this set

- Patch 1.0.1 slowed base stamina regeneration and increased Evil Presence stamina costs. This trio balances lower stamina drain (Unwinded), faster recharge windows (Adrenaline), and better awareness (Sixth Sense).

On field — quick steps

1. Use Sixth Sense to pick safer routes away from Shape Jump audio cues.
2. Conserve stamina with Unwinded during open running; rely on Adrenaline to top up between bursts.
3. If grabbed frequently, place Slippery in the deck to ease the grab-struggle minigame.

### Escape / Discovery build

- Survival Intuition, Exit Strategy, Adrenaline or Unwinded; add Panic Contagion if you free Residents often.

Why this set

- Survival Intuition and Exit Strategy reduce the time spent searching for escape routes. A stamina perk (Adrenaline or Unwinded) keeps you able to sprint once you find an escape. Panic Contagion turns rescue play into movement advantage when you already rescue often.

On field — quick steps

1. Let Survival Intuition reveal an escape option early and use Exit Strategy to extend discovery range when checking rooms.
2. Save stamina bursts for the final route; use Adrenaline if you need faster recharge to chain sprints.
3. If you frequently escort Residents to safety, Panic Contagion can convert rescue actions into a speed window.

### Chase / Anti-Michael build

- Ghost, Elusory, Sixth Sense (Slippery or Knockout as situational swaps).

Why this set

- Patch 1.0.1 increased the penalties from Stalk effects. Ghost and Elusory address Stalk pressure, while Sixth Sense helps you avoid surprise Shape Jumps. Slippery helps escape grabs; Knockout helps teammates extend a knockdown window.

On field — quick steps

1. Stay aware with Sixth Sense; avoid predictable routes into Shape Jump angles.
2. Use Ghost and Elusory to reduce Stalk pressure and preserve more opportunities to recover stamina.
3. If the match becomes grab-heavy, prioritize Slippery; if coordinated knockdowns occur, prioritize Knockout when assisting.

### Objective / Repair build

- Repair Expert, Trespasser, Scavenger (add Exit Strategy or Convincing Plea for routing/support).

Why this set

- Repair Expert reduces kit-consumption risk, Trespasser opens locked routes for objective approaches, and Scavenger improves hiding-spot rummage progress. Exit Strategy or Convincing Plea helps with escape-route discovery or Police timing when appropriate.

On field — quick steps

1. Use Trespasser to bypass locked doors and shorten routes to objectives.
2. Start repairs with Repair Expert to reduce kit-loss risk; use Scavenger when searching hiding spots.
3. If your objective path benefits from knowing escape routes, add Exit Strategy; if you need to influence Police timing, consider Convincing Plea.

### Residents / Police support build

- Pied Piper, Shared Desperation, Shield Of Law (add Frantic or Convincing Plea when handling Police calls).

Why this set

- These cards support different team tasks: Pied Piper increases follower capacity, Shared Desperation improves the damage of Residents you arm, and Shield Of Law reduces your Fear gain near a Police officer. Frantic and Convincing Plea are situational when you are actively calling or coordinating Police presence.

On field — quick steps

1. Prioritize arming Residents and placing them where Shared Desperation can matter.
2. Use Shield Of Law when you are working near a Police officer and need to manage your own Fear gain.
3. If you make or complete Police calls, use Frantic or Convincing Plea according to the confirmed effect direction.

## Why Patch 1.0.1 changes stamina-perk priority

### Confirmed patch changes

- Base stamina regeneration delay increased.
- Evil Presence stamina consumption increased.
- Stalk now reduces stamina regeneration and increases regeneration delay.
- High Athleticism stamina and regeneration effects were weakened.

### Practical implications (editorial)

- Stamina recovery timing matters more when the game imposes longer delays and stronger Stalk penalties.
- Adrenaline and Unwinded become practical priorities for players who need repeated movement bursts, while Ghost and Elusory matter more to players repeatedly targeted by Stalk.
- These are role recommendations, not exact numeric advantages. Reprioritize based on how often you are Stalked or need repeated sprints.

## Situational perks worth considering

- Slippery — strong when grabs are the primary failure point for you.
- Knockout — useful when your team coordinates consistent knockdowns.
- Panic Contagion — only valuable if you regularly complete Resident rescues and can use the resulting movement window.
- Convincing Plea and Frantic — depend on whether you are actively playing the Police-call/support role.
- Pied Piper and Shared Desperation — team-composition dependent; valuable when you plan to shepherd or arm Residents.
- Trespasser — most relevant on maps with frequent locked doors; otherwise situational.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a single stamina perk as a universal fix — Patch 1.0.1 makes the timing of regeneration and Stalk interactions important, not just raw stamina numbers.
- Assuming card names or same-name Traits behave identically or stack predictably — stacking behaviors and exact activation counts are not established.
- Overloading a deck with only detection or escape perks and neglecting objective or support needs — role and map context matter.
- Using Repair Expert as a guarantee to preserve kits — it provides a chance, not certainty.
- Expecting any build to guarantee escape, arrest, or a particular outcome — card effects do not create guaranteed match results.
