# Halloween: The Game Characters — Playable Civilian Roster

**Confirmed:** Halloween: The Game's Standard Edition includes **10 playable Civilians** at launch. Each Civilian has **unique stats, special traits, and personality** according to IllFonic—but full numeric tables and tier lists have not been published pre-launch.

## How many playable Civilians are there?

- **Standard Edition:** 10 Civilians (6 new + 4 legacy film characters)
- **Digital Deluxe exclusive:** Richard and Alexis (2 additional Civilians)

Michael Myers is the Killer role in asymmetric multiplayer; he is not part of the Civilian roster.

## New characters (Standard Edition)

IllFonic's "Heroes of Haddonfield" posts confirm these original Civilians:

- **Jennifer**
- **Tanya**
- **Rachel**
- **Eric**
- **Marcus**
- **Thomas**

## Legacy characters (Standard Edition)

Reimagined film characters confirmed for Standard Edition:

- **Bob Simms**
- **Lynda van der Klok**
- **Annie Brackett**
- **Laurie Strode**

**Yes—Laurie Strode and Annie Brackett are playable Civilians** in the launch roster.

## Deluxe exclusive Civilians

The Digital Deluxe Edition adds two exclusive Civilians:

- **Richard**
- **Alexis**

See /standard-vs-deluxe-upgrade/ for edition and upgrade details.

## Do characters have different stats or traits?

**Confirmed:** IllFonic states every Civilian has unique stats, special traits, and personality. The developer has not published complete stat sheets, perk trees, or balance patches ahead of launch.
