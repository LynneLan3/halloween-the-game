### Chapter 4 Challenges — Quick Answer (Challenges 3, 5, 6)

Quick answer: What is known from community and video evidence—

- Challenge 3: The target is Lynda's telephone execution while Michael is wearing the Sheet. In the successful runs reviewed the observed route was: kill Bob first, collect Bob's glasses, obtain the upstairs Sheet and keep it equipped, then perform the Lynda telephone execution (this route is an observed sequence, not proven to be a universal requirement).  
- Challenge 5: Players report the challenge requires fully stalking Bob and Lynda while they make out on the living-room couch; the living-room closet is a reported viewing position. Multiple reports show both Bob and Lynda reaching Level 3 on the couch before leaving is associated with registration (this is reported/observed, not confirmed as a hard requirement).  
- Challenge 6: Runs following the convergent film-plot sequence (dog/laundry trigger → Annie car kill → Bob/Lynda stalking → Bob wall kill → Sheet telephone kill → body staging → Laurie discovery → final Laurie attack/cutscene) have been used to attempt Challenge 6. Players report Challenge 6 sometimes fails to register even after movie-accurate runs; Lynda AI movement, Annie timing, and Laurie closet selection are named as possible failure points. Closing the bedroom door before killing Bob is a player-reported workaround intended to keep Lynda upstairs. Official Hotfix 1 states several challenge-tracking issues were fixed but does not say which challenges or prove Chapter 4 tracking is fully reliable.

If a challenge does not register, record the run (see "What to record" below) and try the troubleshooting steps listed further down.

Troubleshooting-first diagnostic (symptom → what it may mean → next action)

- Symptom: Challenge does not register after what appears to be a correct run.  
  - What it may mean: A missed hidden state (NPC levels/timing), a player workaround affecting NPC placement, or an Early Access tracking/AI issue reported by the community. Hotfix 1 may have fixed some problems, but it does not guarantee Chapter 4 is fully reliable.  
  - Next action: Re-run the observed sequence (steps below); if it still fails, record full footage (see "What to record") and report with timestamps. Try the suggested workaround (closing the bedroom door) as a repeatable test to see if it changes NPC placement.

- Symptom: Lynda leaves upstairs / does not stay on expected route.  
  - What it may mean: Community reports list Lynda AI movement as a possible failure point for Challenge 6.  
  - Next action: Attempt the run while using the bedroom-door workaround to keep Lynda upstairs (see Workarounds section); if that changes results, capture and compare footage.

- Symptom: Bob or Lynda leave the couch before seeming to reach Level 3.  
  - What it may mean: Players report both NPCs reaching Level 3 before leaving the couch is associated with Challenge 5 registration, but it is unknown whether this is a hard requirement.  
  - Next action: Repeat the stalking until both reach Level 3 on the couch (as reported), then attempt to complete the rest of the sequence and record whether the challenge registers.

- Mistake: Not recording platform/build/hotfix. Quick check: If a run fails to register, without build/hotfix info the report is harder to diagnose — include it.
