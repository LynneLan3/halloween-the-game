# Halloween: The Game — Standard vs Digital Deluxe: Worth the $20? Upgrade & Missables

Quick answer

- Price and value: Standard is $39.99 USD; Digital Deluxe is $59.99 USD — a $20 difference. Digital Deluxe adds the Early Access path plus the Inmate Michael Myers skin, the civilians Richard and Alexis, the Deluxe comic and other preorder/Deluxe extras.
- Upgrade path: A Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU will be available at launch so Standard buyers can add Deluxe content later; the upgrade price is unannounced and must not be assumed to equal $20.
- Early Access & preorder timing: Early Access (Advance Access) begins Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT; full launch is Tuesday, Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. Official clarification says purchasing Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window still grants Early Access and preorder bonuses through Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT — see [Advance Access timing]({{page:early-access-release-time}}).
- Missable item: The Phantom Michael Myers skin is a preorder-only item and is not restored by the Deluxe Upgrade.

Which edition should you buy — quick recommendation

- Buy Digital Deluxe ($59.99) if:
  - You want Early Access on Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT and immediate access to Deluxe cosmetics/characters.
  - You value the included Deluxe items (Inmate Michael, Richard, Alexis) enough to pay $20.
  - You want to guarantee preorder bonuses available during the Early Access window.
- Buy Standard ($39.99) if:
  - You only care about the base game and plan to play at or after full launch (Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT).
  - You prefer to delay spending and accept that preorder-only items (like Phantom) will be lost.
- Unsure: buy Standard now and upgrade at launch if needed — the Deluxe Upgrade SKU will let you add Deluxe content later, but the upgrade price is unannounced and preorder-only items remain missable.

Supporting detail

Upgrade details — how upgrading later works

1. At launch there will be a Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU that lets Standard buyers add the Deluxe bundle without repurchasing the full Digital Deluxe edition.
2. The Upgrade grants the Inmate Michael Myers skin, the civilians Richard and Alexis, and the other Deluxe extras described in the official preorder FAQ.
3. The price for the Deluxe Upgrade is unannounced — do not assume it equals the $20 price difference between the full SKUs.
4. The Phantom Michael Myers preorder skin is not restored by the Deluxe Upgrade. If Phantom matters to you, you must acquire it during the preorder / Early Access window.

Early Access and timing (what to expect)

- Advance Access schedule: Early Access begins Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT; full launch is Tuesday, Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT. See [Advance Access timing]({{page:early-access-release-time}}).
- Important clarification: Purchasing Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window still grants Early Access and preorder bonuses through Sep 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT.
- What this does not do: Buying a Deluxe Upgrade after the Early Access window ends does not retroactively give you an Early Access window. The upgrade adds Deluxe content going forward only.

Missable items and the Phantom skin

- Phantom Michael Myers is explicitly a Digital pre-order exclusive. It will not be made available via the Deluxe Upgrade after the preorder/Early Access cutoff described by IllFonic.
- If a preorder-only cosmetic (Phantom) is a deciding factor for you, purchase Digital Deluxe before the preorder/Early Access cutoff.

Common mistakes to avoid

- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade will grant the Phantom skin — it will not.
- Assuming Standard includes Early Access — Standard does not list Early Access.
- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade price is already known or equal to the $20 full-edition difference — the upgrade price is unannounced.
- Assuming any post–Sep 8 behavior (like restoring Early Access or preorder items) beyond official timings — do not infer post-launch policy.

What happens next

- Want Sep 4 Early Access or Phantom? Preorder/purchase Digital Deluxe during the Early Access window to secure Early Access and any preorder bonuses.
- Bought Standard and now want Deluxe? At launch, buy the Deluxe Edition Upgrade SKU to add Inmate Michael, Richard & Alexis, and other Deluxe extras; the Phantom preorder-only skin will remain unavailable if you missed the preorder window.
