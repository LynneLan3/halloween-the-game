# Halloween: The Game Offline Bots & Private Matches: What's Confirmed

Quick answer

- IllFonic has officially announced bot play and private matches against AI.
- The game's original reveal copy explicitly described playing against bots offline.
- Private matches with friends (hostable private lobbies with role selection/randomization) have been announced.
- Online multiplayer remains a 1v4 player structure.
- The Advance Access / final-build implementation details for bot/private-AI matches (menus, exact online/offline behavior, bot counts/difficulty, reward/XP rules) have not yet been verified.

What IllFonic Has Confirmed About Bots

- Single-player Story Mode exists as a separate, standalone solo experience.
- IllFonic's Progression & Customization update explicitly lists private matches against AI as a distinct play option alongside Story Mode and online 1v4 matchmaking.
- Private matches with friends are part of the official matchmaking overview; hosts can set or randomize roles.
[IMAGE: Progression & Customization update announcement screenshot]

Offline Bots vs Private Matches Against AI

- Original reveal wording described playing against bots offline — that language appears in early official announcements and contemporaneous coverage.
- IllFonic's later Progression & Customization update moves beyond the early wording by explicitly listing private matches against AI as an official option.
- What is not yet verified: whether the private-AI matches are fully playable without any network connection, and the exact menu path or requirements to start them. The Advance Access / launch build will confirm those behavior details.

Private Matches With Friends

- IllFonic's official matchmaking overview confirms hosts can create private matches with friends and that role assignment in private matches can be specified or randomized.
- This is an officially announced feature; private friend lobbies are not only third‑party rumor.

What Is Still Not Confirmed

The following implementation details have not been verified in Advance Access or retail builds:

- Exact menu path to start private-AI matches or offline bot games
- Which side(s) (killer or victim/other roles) can be played against AI
- Whether specific killers (including Michael) can be AI-controlled
- Bot count per match and bot difficulty options
- Available bot settings and customization
- Whether any bot play guarantees a no-network/no-internet launch
- Whether private matches automatically fill empty player slots with bots
- Whether public matchmaking uses AI backfill
- Whether disconnected human players are replaced by AI
- Exact XP, progression, or reward behavior for bot/private-AI matches

Bots vs Story Mode

- Story Mode is a separate single-player experience and is not the same feature as private matches against AI.
- For Story Mode details, see the single-player hub: [single-player hub]({{page:single-player-hub}}).

Launch-Build Verification

- IllFonic's official announcements establish that bot/private-AI play is a planned, official feature, but the Advance Access / launch build is required to verify the exact in-game implementation.
- The specific items listed in "What Is Still Not Confirmed" will be checked once Advance Access is live and IllFonic publishes launch-build notes.
- Follow official IllFonic channels and the game's homepage for verification and patch notes: [Official homepage]({{hub}}).

Common mistakes

- Assuming "offline bots" are already verified in the Advance Access or retail build — they are officially announced but the final implementation is unverified.
- Treating Story Mode and private-AI matches as the same mode — they are separate experiences.
- Assuming private matches automatically backfill with bots or replace disconnected players with AI — those behaviors are unconfirmed.
- Interpreting the word "offline" as a guarantee of play without any internet connection — the announcement described offline bot play historically, but final connection behavior is not yet verified.

What happens next

- IllFonic has published the Progression & Customization and matchmaking overview updates that list private matches against AI and private friend lobbies. The next concrete verification step will be Advance Access / launch-build testing and any accompanying patch notes or FAQs from IllFonic.
- Watch IllFonic's official news posts and the game's homepage for the Advance Access verification and specific menu/setting details: [Official homepage]({{hub}}).
