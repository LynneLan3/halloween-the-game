# Halloween: The Game matchmaking queue — Roles, Tokens & Private Matches

Quick answer
- Online matches are 1 Michael vs 4 Civilians (up to 5 players total).  
- Press coverage of IllFonic’s matchmaking overview confirms you can host private matches with friends; private-match roles can be specified or randomized.  
- IllFonic lists private matches against AI in its official materials.  
- Secondary press reporting (Playfront) describes a role-based system with separate Killer/Civilian queues plus a flexible “no-preference” option and a purchaselike “Killer Token” priority for the Michael queue — label that as secondary reporting, not an official FAQ.  
- What remains unknown from official materials: whether public matchmaking uses AI backfill and whether private lobbies auto-fill empty slots with bots.

Role queues
- Secondary coverage (Playfront) reports IllFonic is using role-based matchmaking: separate Killer and Civilian queues and a flexible/no-preference search that can match players into either role. Treat this as reporting from a secondary outlet rather than an official IllFonic FAQ.  
- The role-based approach described in secondary reporting is intended to let players queue specifically as Michael or as a Civilian; the same Playfront report also describes an option to search without specifying a role. (See Sources below.)

Killer Tokens (secondary-reported)
- Playfront reports a “Killer Token” priority system for players who want preferential access to the Michael queue. According to that report:
  - Tokens are earned by completing matches (Playfront).
  - You redeem tokens before starting a search to get priority for matching as Michael.
  - Tokens are deducted only when a match actually starts with you as Michael.
  - Lobby disconnects reportedly do not spend tokens.  
- Important: treat all Killer Token details here as secondary reporting (Playfront) of the matchmaking overview. These token mechanics are not presented here as an official IllFonic policy or full FAQ — they are reported descriptions and should be confirmed with IllFonic updates.

Private matches and role assignment
- IllFonic’s matchmaking overview (reported in press coverage) confirms private matches with friends are supported, and hosts can choose whether roles are specified or randomized for those private games. This was announced in the matchmaking overview coverage.  
- IllFonic’s official Progression & Customization overview explicitly lists private matches against AI as a play option.  
- What you can be sure of from official/press material: private matches exist, they can be against AI, and the private-match role assignment can be set or randomized. Nothing in the official materials cited here confirms whether private lobbies automatically fill empty slots with bots — that remains unknown.

What is still unknown (officially)
- Whether public matchmaking will use AI backfill for missing players.  
- Whether private lobbies will automatically fill empty slots with bots.  
- Any ranked vs casual queue structure or ranked ladder details.  
- Exact live wait-time performance or token earn rates/caps (token economy numbers were not provided in the cited reporting and must not be invented).

Common mistakes to avoid
- Assuming public matchmaking currently uses AI backfill — this has not been confirmed.  
- Assuming private lobbies auto-fill with bots — IllFonic lists private matches against AI as an option, but automatic bot fill for empty private slots is unannounced.  
- Treating Playfront’s wording as an IllFonic FAQ — Playfront is secondary reportage and should be treated as such, especially for token economy specifics.

What happens next
- Expect IllFonic to clarify token mechanics, backfill behavior, and queue details in future official updates or a matchmaking FAQ. For now, use the official Progression & Customization and multiplayer overviews for confirmed features and watch publisher/developer channels for changes.
