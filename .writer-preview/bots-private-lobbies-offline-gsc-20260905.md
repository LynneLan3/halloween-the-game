# Does Halloween: The Game Have Offline Bots? — Yes (Private AI Matches Confirmed)

Quick answer

- Yes — IllFonic has officially announced private matches against AI.
- The developer’s Progression & Customization overview lists private matches against AI alongside Story Mode and online 1v4 matchmaking.
- Early reveal/storefront wording also described “playing against bots offline,” but that wording alone does not prove fully network‑disconnected play in live builds.
- Private matches with friends (hosted private lobbies and crossplay friend invites) are covered in live‑period reliable media.
- Fully network‑offline / zero‑internet bot play in the shipping / Advance Access build remains not confirmed by live‑build evidence.
- Story Mode is a separate single‑player campaign and is distinct from private AI matches.

What IllFonic has confirmed about bots / AI

- IllFonic’s Progression & Customization overview explicitly lists three play options: singleplayer Story Mode, private matches against AI, and online 1v4 matchmaking. This is the primary official confirmation that private AI matches are an intended feature. ([Progression & Customization overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/progression-customization-overview))
- The game’s earlier reveal/storefront messaging used phrasing that described playing against bots offline; that language is part of the public announcement record but does not by itself establish the connection/online requirements of the live build. ([Gamespot reveal coverage](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/halloween-the-game-coming-from-friday-the-13th-devs-features-single-player/1100-6534148/), [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3219630/Halloween/))
- Official materials also confirm the core online multiplayer format remains structured as 1v4 matchmaking.

Offline bots vs private matches against AI vs true offline mode (boundary)

- Confirmed: private matches against AI are an official play option in IllFonic’s messaging.
- Clarification: the presence of “offline” wording in earlier copy and announcements indicates the developer described bot play in those terms, but reliable live‑build evidence has not confirmed whether private AI matches can run with zero network connection in practice.
- Practical takeaway: treat “private matches against AI” as confirmed; treat “fully network‑offline/no‑internet play” as not verified until developers or live builds explicitly confirm it.

Private matches with friends

- Private lobbies and friend invites are covered by reliable live‑period reporting. TheGamer’s multiplayer guide (Sep 4, 2026) describes creating private matches and using crossplay friend codes to invite friends; that coverage treats private matches with friends as supported in the live period. ([TheGamer — How To Play With Friends](https://www.thegamer.com/halloween-the-game-multiplayer-friends-crossplay-guide/))
- Official messaging presents private matches (including private AI matches) as distinct from public 1v4 matchmaking and from Story Mode.

What is still not confirmed (items to verify in live builds)

Do not assume any of the following until verified in a live build or confirmed by the developer:

- That private AI matches run entirely without any network connection (true offline/no‑internet play).
- Exact menu paths, UI labels, or how to access private AI matches.
- Which side(s) (killer or survivor) can be controlled by AI, or whether specific killer characters are available as AI.
- Bot counts, difficulty options, behavior details, or whether private lobbies auto‑fill with bots.
- Whether public matchmaking uses AI backfill for disconnects.
- Any XP, reward, or progression differences for matches that include bots.

Common mistakes players make reading pre‑release posts

- Treating private AI matches as mere rumor — incorrect. IllFonic lists private matches against AI in official materials.
- Conflating Story Mode with private AI matches — Story Mode is listed separately as a single‑player campaign experience.
- Assuming the word “offline” in reveal copy guarantees zero‑network play at launch — connection behavior remains unverified in live builds.
- Assuming public matchmaking will automatically use AI backfill or private lobbies will auto‑fill with bots — neither behavior is confirmed.

Bots vs Story Mode

- Story Mode is a dedicated single‑player campaign separate from private AI matches and the developer’s bot wording. For the single‑player campaign details, see [{{page:single-player-hub}}]({{page:single-player-hub}}).
