How to Arrest Michael Myers in Halloween: The Game
Quick Answer
- You cannot simply kill Michael Myers in multiplayer. Instead the match can escalate police/reinforcement pressure until a detainment window opens: Michael will show a handcuff-style indicator (a diamond/icon with handcuffs) when arrest readiness is building; after that, knock Michael down, press the shown [E] Detain Michael prompt, finish the short circular progress/input minigame, and the match ends with MICHAEL DETAINED! — Michael is returned to Smith’s Grove Sanitarium and remaining Civilians do not need a separate escape. (Sources: official multiplayer overview; gameplay footage — Bryce Games @ 2:18–2:38; D3AD Plays @ 7:44.)

Can you kill Michael Myers?
- No. Official materials and gameplay footage confirm Michael cannot simply be killed in multiplayer; he is the unkillable Boogeyman and can be detained and returned to Smith’s Grove Sanitarium when the community triggers the arrest sequence. (Official overview + gameplay footage.)

How the arrest system works (high level)
- Police/reinforcements escalate through Civilian calls and reinforcement progression; HUD messages observed include strings like A COP HAS ARRIVED TO INVESTIGATE and Reinforcements Available / Reinforcements In during matches.
- Dead or escaped Civilians can still support the match: they may return as a Sheriff’s Deputy or Dr. Loomis. Spectator-side Police Reinforcements perks can respawn a player as a police officer who contributes to arrest pressure.
- Arrest readiness shows as a handcuff-style indicator (a diamond/icon with handcuffs) on or near Michael while police pressure is building.
- Only after arrest readiness is established does a knockdown produce the usable detain window. Knocking Michael down earlier will let him recover — those early knockdowns do not complete the detainment sequence.
- Once down in the ready state, the interaction prompt [E] Detain Michael appears with a handcuff icon. Completing the Detain interaction requires finishing a short circular progress/input interaction before Michael recovers. A successful Detain ends the match with MICHAEL DETAINED! (GAME OVER / You Survived The Night).

Step-by-step arrest sequence (player order)
Follow these steps in player order; they match observed gameplay sequences and official overview claims.

1. Create/allow police pressure to build
   - Encourage Civilian calls or use interactions that summon authority support (gameplay footage shows CB / shortwave radio being used in arrest runs).
   - Note: Dead or escaped Civilians can still re-enter as a Deputy or Dr. Loomis; a Police Reinforcements perk can respawn a spectator as a police officer who increases arrest pressure.

2. Watch for HUD escalation and the handcuff readiness marker
   - Observe for reinforcement HUD messages (e.g., A COP HAS ARRIVED TO INVESTIGATE; Reinforcements Available / Reinforcements In) as police pressure rises.
   - When arrest readiness is building, Michael will show a handcuff-style indicator (diamond/icon with handcuffs) on or near him.
3. Wait for the ready state before performing the decisive knockdown
   - Do not rely on early knockdowns; footage shows Michael can recover from a knockdown if the handcuff/readiness state is not established.
4. Deliver the knockdown while Michael has the handcuff readiness marker
   - After the handcuff indicator appears, a knockdown becomes the usable opportunity for detainment.
5. Interact to Detain Michael
   - After a valid knockdown in the ready state, the prompt [E] Detain Michael appears with a handcuff icon. Press [E].

6. Complete the short circular progress / input minigame
   - Finish the short circular progress/input interaction before Michael recovers. The minigame was observed in gameplay footage as a circular progress element that must complete to lock the detain.
7. Match result on success
   - A successful Detain triggers the end state MICHAEL DETAINED! (GAME OVER / You Survived The Night). Remaining Civilians do not need to escape separately once Detain completes.

Arrest meter / handcuff state (what to look for)
- Visual marker: When readiness is building, Michael displays a handcuff-style indicator (a diamond/icon with handcuffs) on or near him — this is the key visual cue that police pressure and the arrest meter are at a meaningful level.
- Timing: Knocking Michael down before that readiness/handcuff marker appears is insufficient — footage shows he can recover from those knockdowns and the detainment will not proceed.
- Detain prompt: After a valid knockdown in the ready state the [E] Detain Michael prompt appears with a handcuff icon; pressing it starts the short circular progress/in‑game input that must complete to finish the Detain.

What the Detain prompt and minigame look like
- Observed visuals show an on-screen [E] Detain Michael prompt with a handcuff icon when Michael is knocked down in the ready state.
- Completing Detain requires finishing a short circular progress/input interaction while Michael is down. If the progress fails or is interrupted before completion, Michael can recover and the arrest fails.

What happens after MICHAEL DETAINED
- A successful detainment ends the match with a MICHAEL DETAINED! end state (GAME OVER / You Survived The Night).
- Remaining Civilians do not need a separate escape afterward — the detain conclusion is the match-ending result.
- Official material states Michael is returned to Smith’s Grove Sanitarium after successful detainment.

Common mistakes players make
- Trying to Detain immediately after any knockdown — many early knockdowns happen before arrest readiness and will let Michael recover.
- Failing to watch for the handcuff indicator; attempt Detain only when that diamond/handcuff icon is present or when HUD reinforcement messages indicate police pressure.
- Starting the Detain interaction and not finishing the circular progress — interruption lets Michael recover.
