# Halloween: The Game — Standard vs Deluxe: Which Edition Should You Buy?

Quick answer
- Standard ($39.99) is the safe buy if you only want the base game and core multiplayer.  
- Digital Deluxe ($59.99) buys you a Sep 4 Advance Access preorder window plus the Deluxe extras (Inmate Michael Myers skin, Richard & Alexis civilians, and other preorder bonuses).  
- You can buy Standard now and purchase the Deluxe Upgrade at launch to add Richard, Alexis, and the Inmate skin — but the Phantom Michael Myers preorder skin is strictly preorder‑exclusive and will not be available from the post‑launch upgrade. The Deluxe Upgrade price is unannounced.

[IMAGE: edition-boxes-and-skins-side-by-side]

## Confirmed (kept from official sources)

- Editions and prices: Standard — **$39.99 USD**; Digital Deluxe — **$59.99 USD** (official FAQ and storefront listings).
- Digital Deluxe includes: Base game, **Inmate Michael Myers skin**, civilians **Richard** and **Alexis**, comic and other preorder extras.
- Phantom vs Inmate skins: **Phantom** is a **digital pre-order exclusive** and is described as **not available after the pre-order period**. **Inmate** is part of Digital Deluxe and is available through the **Deluxe upgrade at launch**.
- Upgrade path at launch: IllFonic's FAQ confirms a **Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade** will be available at launch and grants the civilians and the Inmate skin; the FAQ explicitly notes the **Phantom pre-order skin is not restored** by that upgrade.
- Advance Access timing: Digital Deluxe preorders grant Advance Access beginning **Friday, September 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT**. The Standard preorder listing does **not** list the same Advance Access bullet.
- Single-player / offline bots: Official product copy lists a single-player story mode and offline play against bots as separate from online multiplayer.

## Which edition should you buy — conditional recommendation

- If you only want the full base game and core multiplayer (no need for early access or Deluxe extras): buy Standard ($39.99). You can always upgrade at launch to add Deluxe extras.
- If you want guaranteed Sep 4 Advance Access and the preorder-exclusive Phantom skin: preorder Digital Deluxe ($59.99) before the preorder window closes.
- If you want Richard, Alexis, and the Inmate skin but don't care about Phantom or Advance Access: buy Standard now and purchase the Deluxe Upgrade at launch (price unannounced).
- If you're undecided: buy Standard now (safer). You keep the option to upgrade at launch, but you will permanently miss Phantom if you do not preorder.

No marketing language: these are the trade-offs based on official FAQ and storefront listings.

## Can you upgrade later? (what to do, step by step)
1. Purchase Standard or pre-order it through your chosen storefront.  
2. At launch, purchase the Digital Deluxe Upgrade SKU from the same storefront to add Deluxe content to a Standard copy — storefront listings already show a Deluxe upgrade SKU in some cases. (Exact upgrade price per storefront is **Not Announced**.)  
3. After upgrading, you will receive the Deluxe civilians (Richard & Alexis) and the Inmate Michael Myers skin as confirmed by the FAQ.  
4. Do not expect the Phantom Michael Myers preorder skin to be added by the upgrade — official materials say Phantom is preorder‑exclusive and is not restored by the upgrade.  
5. Unknown: whether buying the upgrade after preorder cutoffs grants the Sep 4 Advance Access — IllFonic has not announced this.

## Early access (exact, confirmed)
- Digital Deluxe preorders grant Advance Access starting **Friday, September 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT** (confirmed).  
- Standard preorder listings do **not** include the same Advance Access bullet (confirmed).  
- IllFonic has not confirmed whether the Deluxe Upgrade purchased at or after launch will retroactively grant the Sep 4 Advance Access window — treat that as Not Announced.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Assuming the post‑launch Deluxe Upgrade restores the Phantom preorder skin — it does not. Phantom is preorder‑exclusive.  
- Assuming the Deluxe Upgrade price is known — storefront prices for the upgrade are **Not Announced** in official materials.  
- Assuming only Deluxe has single‑player or offline options — official product copy lists single‑player and offline bot play separately from Deluxe content; the base game includes offline options.  
- Assuming upgrading before Sep 4 guarantees early access — IllFonic has only confirmed Advance Access for Digital Deluxe preorders; whether upgrades or late Deluxe purchases get Sep 4 access is Not Announced.

## What happens next (timeline)
1. Preorder Digital Deluxe before the preorder cutoff → you get Advance Access on **Sep 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT** and the Phantom preorder skin.  
2. If you miss the preorder window, full launch is **Sep 8, 2026** and the base game becomes available for everyone.  
3. Standard owners can purchase the Deluxe Upgrade at or after launch to add Richard, Alexis, and the Inmate skin; Phantom will remain unavailable unless you had preordered.  
4. Monitor official storefront pages or IllFonic FAQ for the announced Deluxe Upgrade price and any change to early access rules.
